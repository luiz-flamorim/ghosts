Mapping the Unseen

Mapping the Unseen is a visualisation that plots reported ghost sightings based on historical records, eyewitness accounts, and documented investigations, using data extracted from The Paranormal Database. The site allows users to explore haunted locations near them, offering a self-guided way to navigate the eerie history of their surroundings.

Ghost sightings within approximately 2 km of your location will be highlighted on the map. You can pan and zoom around the map to explore different areas, or use the search bar to enter a postcode and find reports in specific locations. Hover over a ghost marker to highlight its corresponding entry in the list, or click on a circle to reveal more details. You can also click on a ghost’s name in the list to open a detailed view, which includes an AI-generated image representing the sighting.

The generated images are created using **AI-based visual interpretations** of each ghost story, aiming to capture the eerie essence of the reports. These images do not represent real photographs but rather **imaginative reconstructions** inspired by the descriptions found in the database.

By making this data accessible and interactive, Mapping the Unseen transforms ghost stories into a mapped experience, bridging folklore, machine-generated visuals, and location-based discovery.
