Mapping the Unseen

Mapped the Unseen is a visualisation that plots reported ghost sightings based on historical records, eyewitness accounts, and documented investigations, based on the data extracted from The Paranormal Database. The site allows users to explore haunted locations near them, offering a self-guided way to navigate the eerie history of their surroundings. By making this data accessible and interactive, Mapped the Unseen transforms ghost stories into a mapped experience, bridging folklore and location-based discovery.
